Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Learn how we protect and use your personal data.

Last updated: October 8, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Bizzy OPS ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our website and services. We are committed to protecting your privacy in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

We may collect the following personal information:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, company name
  • Technical Information: IP address, browser type, operating system
  • Service Information: Details about your IT infrastructure and requirements
  • Communication Records: Emails, chat messages, and support tickets
  • Usage Data: How you interact with our website and services

1.2 How We Collect Information

  • When you contact us through our website forms
  • When you request services or support
  • During the provision of IT services
  • Through cookies and similar technologies
  • When you interact with our live chat feature

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 Legitimate Business Purposes

We use your personal information for:

  • Service Delivery: Providing IT support, maintenance, and consulting services
  • Communication: Responding to inquiries and providing customer support
  • Billing: Processing payments and maintaining financial records
  • Improvement: Analyzing service usage to improve our offerings
  • Legal Compliance: Meeting regulatory and legal obligations
  • Security: Protecting our systems and your data from threats

2.2 Marketing Communications

With your consent, we may send you:

  • Service updates and security alerts
  • Educational content and best practices
  • Information about new services

You can opt out of marketing communications at any time using the unsubscribe link or by contacting us.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data based on:

  • Contract Performance: To fulfill our service agreements with you
  • Legitimate Interest: To operate our business and improve our services
  • Consent: For marketing communications and optional services
  • Legal Obligation: To comply with applicable laws and regulations

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

4.1 Third-Party Service Providers

We may share your information with trusted third parties who assist us in:

  • Cloud hosting and infrastructure services
  • Payment processing
  • Email and communication services
  • Analytics and website optimization
  • Customer support tools

4.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information when required by law, court order, or to:

  • Protect our legal rights
  • Investigate fraud or security issues
  • Comply with regulatory requirements

4.3 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of the transaction.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data:

  • Encryption: Data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Access Controls: Limited access on a need-to-know basis
  • Regular Audits: Security assessments and vulnerability testing
  • Staff Training: Regular privacy and security training for all employees
  • Incident Response: Procedures for detecting and responding to data breaches

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide ongoing services to you
  • Meet legal and regulatory requirements
  • Resolve disputes and enforce agreements

Typically, we retain:

  • Service Records: For the duration of our relationship plus 6 years
  • Financial Records: 7 years from the end of the financial year
  • Marketing Consent: Until withdrawn or 2 years of inactivity

7. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data in certain circumstances
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Limit how we use your data
  • Right to Data Portability: Request your data in a portable format
  • Right to Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Remove consent for marketing or optional processing

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details provided below.

8. Cookies and Tracking

8.1 Essential Cookies

We use essential cookies that are necessary for our website to function properly. These cannot be disabled.

8.2 Analytics Cookies

We use analytics cookies to understand how visitors use our website and improve user experience.

8.3 Third-Party Services

Our website may include third-party services such as:

  • Google Analytics (with anonymized IP addresses)
  • Live chat functionality (Tawk.to)
  • Contact form services (Web3Forms)

For more details, please see our Cookie Policy.

9. International Transfers

Some of our service providers may be located outside the UK. When transferring data internationally, we ensure adequate protection through:

  • Adequacy decisions by the UK government
  • Standard contractual clauses
  • Other appropriate safeguards as required by law

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16 without parental consent.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. We will notify you of significant changes by email or through our website.

12. Contact Information

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your rights, please contact us:

Email: privacy@bizzyops.com

Address: London, United Kingdom
