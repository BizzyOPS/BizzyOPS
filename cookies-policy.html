Cookie Policy

Learn about the cookies we use and how to manage your cookie preferences.

Last updated: October 8, 2025

This Cookie Policy explains how Bizzy OPS ("we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on our website. By using our website, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to website owners.

1.1 Types of Cookies

  • Session Cookies: Temporary cookies that are deleted when you close your browser
  • Persistent Cookies: Cookies that remain on your device for a set period
  • First-Party Cookies: Set by our website directly
  • Third-Party Cookies: Set by external services we use

2. Cookies We Use

2.1 Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for our website to function properly. They cannot be disabled without affecting the basic functionality of our website.

Cookie Name Purpose Duration

csrf_token Security protection against cross-site request forgery Session
cookie_consent Remembers your cookie preferences 1 year

2.2 Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting anonymous information about usage patterns.

Service Cookie Names Purpose Duration
Google Analytics _ga, _ga_*, _gid Website usage analysis and performance monitoring 2 years / 24 hours

2.3 Functional Cookies

These cookies enhance the functionality of our website and improve your user experience.

These cookies help us optimize website performance and loading times.

3. Third-Party Services

3.1 Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to analyze website usage. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information about how visitors use our site. The information is used to compile reports and help us improve the website.

Data collected includes:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • How visitors arrived at our website
  • Browser and device information (anonymized)
  • General geographic location (country/city level)

Privacy measures: We have enabled IP anonymization, so your full IP address is not stored by Google Analytics.

3.2 Tawk.to Live Chat

Our live chat feature is provided by Tawk.to. This service uses cookies to:

  • Maintain chat sessions
  • Remember previous conversations
  • Provide personalized support
  • Monitor chat performance

3.3 Web3Forms

Our contact forms use Web3Forms for email delivery. This service may use temporary cookies to:

  • Prevent spam submissions
  • Ensure form delivery
  • Maintain form session state

4. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

4.1 Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can:

  • Block all cookies
  • Block third-party cookies only
  • Delete cookies when you close your browser
  • Get notified when cookies are set

4.2 Browser-Specific Instructions

Google Chrome

  1. Click on "Settings" > "Privacy and security" > "Cookies and other site data"
  2. Choose your preferred cookie settings

Mozilla Firefox

  1. Click on "Options" > "Privacy & Security"
  2. Under "Cookies and Site Data," select your preferences

Safari

  1. Click on "Preferences" > "Privacy"
  2. Adjust your cookie settings under "Cookies and website data"

Microsoft Edge

  1. Click on "Settings" > "Cookies and site permissions"
  2. Select "Cookies and site data" to manage preferences

4.3 Opting Out of Analytics

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by:

  • Installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on
  • Enabling "Do Not Track" in your browser settings
  • Using privacy-focused browser extensions

5. Impact of Disabling Cookies

5.1 Essential Cookies

If you disable essential cookies, some parts of our website may not function properly:

  • Contact forms may not submit correctly
  • Security features may be compromised
  • Your preferences may not be saved

5.2 Non-Essential Cookies

Disabling non-essential cookies will:

  • Reduce our ability to improve the website
  • Disable live chat functionality
  • Prevent personalized experiences
  • Make analytics less accurate
